After trying different formats for my blog, such as news stories, personal
essays, a diary, and traditional culture, I couldn’t find anything that really
satisfied me. So I’ve settled on a “freestyle blog” rather than a themed one,
since I don’t really have much free time to come up with stories on a specific
topic.

Now that I’m back home from my trips, I can spare some time to drop a few lines
about various topics, including news, insights, thoughts, and informative posts
about Japan. I’d also like to share my web discoveries with everyone who visits
my blog, like useful links for those interested in learning Japanese, and
interesting websites and blogs. I might also write posts in Japanese from time
to time to practice my writing skills!

それでは、じゃあまた！
