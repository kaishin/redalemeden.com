## Topic I

The _geisha_ plate on my desk gave me the idea to write about them yesterday!
And for those who prefer a professional write-up from the corporate media,
TimesOnline had an article on the topic this morning. To clear up the confusion
about the different apprenticeship levels, I went to the trouble of googling for
the best English resource and stumbled upon this awesome website,
[Geisha Immortal](https://web.archive.org/web/20061024005418/http://www.immortalgeisha.com/index.html).
[Their FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20061029112609/http://www.immortalgeisha.com/faq_geisha.php)
explains the characteristics of _geisha_, _maiko_, and _geiko_, and the
differences between them, in detail.

## Topic II

Mozilla is running a campaign to promote Firefox in Japan. They've created a
cute fox mascot to represent the browser, a move you'd expect in
[the land of the cute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawaii).
[On their website](https://web.archive.org/web/20061108134049/http://spreadfirefox.jp/foxkeh/),
they're running a contest to name the critter. Being a Firefox fanatic, I
couldn't help suggesting a name: フォクシー　(Foxy)! I hope my marketing
instincts will be taken into consideration! (via
[Japan Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20071121203859/http://www.japanprobe.com/?p=458))

## Topic III

[Japan needs nationalism](https://web.archive.org/web/20121024000550/http://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/content/article/2006/08/25/AR2006082501176_pf.html),
but not at the expense of freedom of expression, or so goes an article in the
Washington Post about the rise of Japanese nationalism.

Nationalism, Shinzo Abe, Yasukuni, Junichiro Koizumi… those are the prevailing
keywords in today's Japanese political news. Leftists are panicking as Shinzo
Abe, the favored successor to current PM Junichiro Koizumi, has declared that he
wants to upgrade the Defence Agency to a full ministry, amend Japan's education
laws to boost patriotism in schools, and boost Japan's military presence
overseas. (via
[The Hindu](https://web.archive.org/web/20061105132320/http://www.hindu.com/thehindu/holnus/003200609031551.htm))

Personally, I feel like things are going the way they should, towards building a
[proud Japan](https://web.archive.org/web/20061106083435/http://www3.s-abe.or.jp/),
as written in the background of Abe's website welcome page. Anyway, you can't
expect a different stance from someone who reveres hawks like I do!
