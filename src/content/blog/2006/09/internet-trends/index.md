I apologize for not being able to update my blog every day. Sometimes I just
don't feel like writing anything. I lack the motivation to blog and would rather
practice my _kanji_ or draw some sketches.

Being an internet denizen, I spend more than 60% of my free time surfing. Every
morning, before even having breakfast, I skim more than 20 Japan-related news
websites and blogs. I'm not the type to use social networking services, even
though I have an account on almost all of them, including MySpace.

Speaking of MySpace, I was glad to see that one of my favorite musicians ever,
[Yoshiki](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoshiki_(musician)>), has a
[MySpace account](https://myspace.com/yoshiki) too! He's the former drummer and
pianist of the legendary _visual kei_ band X-JAPAN (一番好きなバンドだよ！), now
pursuing a solo career in Los Angeles.

What prompted me to talk about internet usage and social networking services
(SNS) was this informative article in Metropolis online magazine:
[In the Mix!](https://web.archive.org/web/20061119172057/http://www.metropolis.co.jp/tokyo/650/feature.asp)
The article is about the rising popularity of SNS in Japan, such as Mixi and
other, more topic-focused social networking sites.

Come to think of it, internet trends have changed drastically in recent years,
or more accurately, since 2005. Now I can't imagine the internet without
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Page),
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/), and [Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/). Not
to mention the increasing number of blogs and podcasts. Only now can we really
say that the world is a small village!
