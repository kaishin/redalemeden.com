"Neo" is a prefix derived from Greek for "new," used whenever there is an urge
to convey the freshness of something old that has been revived. I've noticed
that "neo" is being used with unprecedented frequency, in fields ranging from
politics to the arts. It's both handy and worrisome. Refreshing, but lacking
originality. And then, as humanity advances, what will come next? Neo Neo? Ultra
Neo?

Until then, my blog will be called Neo Samurai!
