Let's face it: pink is hot these days, and
[Motorola/NTT Docomo](https://web.archive.org/web/20070522123111/http://www.nttdocomo.co.jp/product/foma/702i/m702is/)
fully endorse my opinion. I couldn't find anyone better than
[little Mirai](https://img.photobucket.com/albums/v335/curiosas/likes/Miraiandherpinkthings.jpg)
to back me up.

Pink is often paired with cuteness, unless you're
[Mori Chack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloomy_Bear) (森チャック). I find his
concepts and designs sublime. One of the most intriguing is the pink Gloomy Bear
in his
[Chax line](https://web.archive.org/web/20070511021327/http://www.chax.net/frameset.html).
According to a press release from
[Cube Works](http://www.cube-works.co.jp/index.html):

> Gloomy, an abandoned little bear, is rescued by Pitty (the little boy). At
> first, he is cute and cuddly, but becomes more wild as he grows up. Since
> bears do not become attached to people like dogs by nature, Gloomy attacks
> Pitty even though he is the owner. So Gloomy has blood on him from biting
> and/or scratching Pitty. The Gloomy with blood is called Chax Colony Edition.

Mori believes that humans and animals can't be friends. He created Gloomy as an
antithesis to the overly cute mascots of
[Sanrio](https://web.archive.org/web/20070510003508/http://www.unicef.org/corporate_partners/images/sanrio_logo.jpg),
Disney…

_PS: This entry is a tribute to a dear friend who loves pink!_
