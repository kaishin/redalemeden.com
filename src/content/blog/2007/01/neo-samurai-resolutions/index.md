My first post in 2007! It took me a week to find time for blogging because I'm
in the middle of my midterm exams. As for my 2007 Neo Samurai resolutions, I've
made up my mind to invest more time and effort in my blog. It's not like it'll
bring me fame or anything, but it's a good way to make new acquaintances and
share my ideas! (It seems I'm still having a blogging identity crisis.)

I also want to make a schedule for my posts, in the form of themed weekly
columns. This way, visitors will know beforehand what to expect from each entry.
The themes will be mythology, history, indie music, Japanese culture for
newbies, funny tidbits, Japan around the web, and a weekly entry about myself.
This structure will help me find enough content to write every day, or at least
once every two days. If any of these topics interest you, please check back
every now and then!
