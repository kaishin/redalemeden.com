I briefly mentioned [Cube Works](http://www.cube-works.co.jp/works/index.html),
a Japanese company that makes everything from stuffed animals to key chains and
figurines, in my [pink entry](/blog/2006/12/pink-is-beautiful/). While browsing
their website tonight, I came across an
[interesting collection](http://www.cube-works.co.jp/works/index_sub.html?/works/historylnquiry/index.html)
of figurines of historical figures… figure it out!

Each figurine is about 10 cm tall and costs ¥504! I'd grab 'em all if I had the
chance!
