_Kotowaza_ (諺, ことわざ) are Japanese proverbs and sayings. Unlike Western
proverbs, _kotowaza_ can take many forms: _inarawashi_ (言い習わし), or short
sayings, which are the closest form to Western proverbs; _kanyouku_ (慣用句), or
idiomatic phrases with meanings hidden behind the literal ones; and _yojijukugo_
(四字熟語), or four-character idioms.

Japanese culture is full of _kotowaza_, and they are frequently used in everyday
conversation, often shortened for simplicity.

Idioms and sayings are the cornerstone of every culture, so understanding them
and the philosophy behind them is the key to fully grasping that culture.
