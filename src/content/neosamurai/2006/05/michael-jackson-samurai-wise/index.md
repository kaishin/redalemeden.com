Michael Jackson landed in Japan yesterday for MTV Japan's "Legend Award," which
takes place today. This is his first public appearance since his acquittal last
June on child molestation charges in the United States. His visit also includes
a trip to an orphanage in Tokyo and meetings with Asian business leaders on
Sunday.

_"I look forward to seeing old friends and saying hello to my huge fan base in
Japan who, like my other fans around the world, have for so many years
consistently shown their love and support to me and my family,"_ MJ said in a
statement.

I wouldn't normally write about this on Neo Samurai, but I came across a funny
photo of MJ in samurai garb.

It was taken in 1987, when I was one year old, during the Japan leg of his "Bad"
tour. I don't know much about MJ and I've never been a fan, but I guess he has
earned his place in pop music history. I can't say whether he looks cool or
uncool in that samurai outfit, but I can say it looks way too staged!
