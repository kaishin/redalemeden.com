I'm still under the spell of the Kikunokai performance, and I'm not about to
forget it anytime soon! So here is a short historical overview of the troupe and
its founder.

Hata Michiyo, born in Kyoto, studied Japanese traditional dance (日本舞踊,
_Nihon Buyou_) under the guidance of Onoe Kikunojo, the first real master of the
Onoe school of dance, founded by the prominent _kabuki_ actor Onoe Kikugoro. In
1972, Hata Michiyo founded the dance troupe
[Kikunokai](/neosamurai/2006/06/kikunokai/), which has been reaping culture and
arts awards ever since.

In 1976, Kikunokai received the Excellent Art Prize at the Cultural Festival
sponsored by the Cultural Agency of Japan.

As a _Shihan_ (master) of Onoe, Hata-san has toured over 22 countries with her
troupe, showcasing the charm and exquisiteness of Japanese folkloric dance. She
gained tremendous fame for her choreography in the film _Dreams_ by the
illustrious director Akira Kurosawa.
