After a week of stressful exams, I decided to go to the Kikunokai
[show in Casablanca](https://web.archive.org/web/20060620004110/http://www.moroccotimes.com/News/article.asp?id=15379)
at all costs. And I did!

[Kikunokai](https://web.archive.org/web/20060615110905/http://www.kikunokai.co.jp/english.html)
is a traditional dance troupe founded by Michiyo Hata in 1972. Hata-san studied
dance for years under the guidance of Onoe Kikunojo I, the first master of the
Onoe dance school, founded by the famed modern _kabuki_ actor Onoe Kikugoro VI.
With Japanese classical dance as their foundation, the members of Kikunokai
perform a variety of dances and other performances, including chants, _taiko_
drumming, and musical comedies.

I really enjoyed the show, particularly the _taiko_ drumming and _Awa-odori_.
They were just amazing!

I'm too tired to write a full-fledged entry, but expect more about this troupe
and their performances in the near future!
