![Kiyomori in samurai armor, standing between two people in armor.](./kiyomori.jpg)

Last time I posted this picture, I talked about Roborior. I've since deleted
that entry because I realized my sources weren't reliable enough. I apologize
for the misunderstanding!

The robot above is called
[Kiyomori](https://web.archive.org/web/20060702091100/http://kiyomori.jp/main.html),
a _samurai_ robot made by Tmusk. It's one of the first prototypes to walk in a
way very similar to humans. I watched the video on the official website, and it
really does seem revolutionary, a step closer to
[Gundam](https://web.archive.org/web/20060506163808/http://smt.blogs.com/mari_diary/2005/05/did_you_go_to_s.html)!

I bet Tmusk are trying to blend Japan's past and future!
