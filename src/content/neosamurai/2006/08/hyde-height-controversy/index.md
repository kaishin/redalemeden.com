Even though I'm not really into the Japanese pop scene, I stumble upon an
interesting story from time to time. Ai Otsuka and Koda Kumi seem to be locked
in a fierce battle to win the _Nihon Rekodo Taisho_, Japan's most prestigious
music award. Even though I'm listening to Koda Kumi right now, it was just on a
whim. I prefer Ai Otsuka's “Planetarium” by far.

Today, while browsing the archives of
[Mari's Watashi to Tokyo](https://web.archive.org/web/20060811213850/http://smt.blogs.com/mari_diary/),
I came across a very funny, though pretty old, story about Hyde, Wikipedia and
the mischievous yet funny 2-channel folks. Hyde is the vocalist of the Japanese
visual… I mean rock band L'Arc~en~Ciel. It's rumored that Hyde (pronounced
_Haido_) is very short. He keeps his real height a secret and has never revealed
it to the media. But some 2-channel folks attacked his Wikipedia entry and
scattered the rumored 156 cm height all over the article in some very funny
ways! The article was modified so many times that it ended up locked, like all
controversial entries.

I went to the trouble of checking the page history and got this wonderful
description of our beloved Hyde. I wanted to copy and paste the whole page, but
I respect Hyde's “privacy”. And even though I like ラルク, I couldn't help
mentioning this story. You can clearly see how many times 156 is mentioned,
sometimes in really weird and funny ways. I'll pick the best ones:

> 156hyde(ひゃくごじゅうろくハイド)は日本の身長156cm5’2”ヴォーカリスト

“156Hyde (one hundred fifty-six Hyde) is Japan's 156 cm (5’2”) vocalist.”

> 身長（実は156cm）、体重、生年月日等のプロフィールを完全に隠して…そして身長が56cmであることを公表した。

“His height (actually 156 cm), weight, birth date and other personal details are
kept completely hidden… Then he officially announced that his height was 56 cm.”

> さらにはドラム、アルトサックスなど多様な楽器を身長156cmながら操ることができる。

“Furthermore, even though he is 156 cm tall, he can play various instruments,
including drums and alto sax.”

> 156年1月56日生まれ

“Born in the year 156, month 1, day 56.”

> 身長は156cm。hyde(身長156cm)は小学生の頃か

“His height is 156 cm. Hyde (height 156 cm), since grade school…”

And here are the different bands Hyde played for, according to the 2-channel
folks:

- 156
- P’UNK～EN～CIEL

- 156センチ (centi)
- 難波ロケッツ

- Gackt

- ニルヴァーナ (Nirvana)
- メタリカ (Metallica)
- 156センチメーター (centimeters)
- サッポロ一番塩ラーメン
- マリリン・マンソン (Marilyn Manson)
- 百五十六 (one hundred fifty-six)
- 山本五十六 (Yamamoto 56)

- 1.56ｍ

- ひゃくごじゅうろくはいど (156 cm _haido_)

The joke is that the English Wikipedia still has this info hidden between the
lines, even now.

Well, I guess _Haido-san_ won't be pleased to see this post, so to atone for it,
I'm going to remove the info from the English Wikipedia. By the way, the French
wiki currently says 158 cm, and the Japanese one gives a broad range from 156 cm
to 160 cm or so, emphasizing that Hyde has never announced it officially.

Special thanks to Mari for the original story!
