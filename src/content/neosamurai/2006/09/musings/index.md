“I don’t know how my story started, nor how it will unfold, much less how it
will end. Day after day, I feel like I don’t belong to this world, that I was
born against the flow of time, in the vast Sahara desert where the horizon hides
the truth lying far beyond the burning sand dunes.

I have been walking barefoot, hoping to find the oasis, only to be tricked by
the mirage over and over again.

‘Where to?’ I uttered, kneeling on the blazing dunes.

My life has been this way since my very first cry, roaming aimlessly, with
nothing in hand but a gourd of despair.

With no landmarks to guide my weak body, my quest to find a destination was even
more challenging. Until the day I realized that the sun rises from the very same
spot every dawn, breaking the chilling loneliness of the Sahara nights.

I couldn’t help being bewitched by the transcendental rising sun. I firmly
resolved to seek its rays, to bear hunger and thirst, and to keep on walking
guided by its daylight.

I spend the nights counting the stars, hoping for the sun to rise again and
light my pathway, thinking that no matter how long the night may get, a new
morning will surely come.

And so my story goes on: I seek the sun and panic at the thought of being
blinded by its radiance!”

_Kaishin, the lost samurai_
