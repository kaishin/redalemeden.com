Amaterasu gave birth to five _kami_, whereas Susanowo gave birth to three
_megami_. After Amaterasu
[declared the division of the newly born deities](/neosamurai/2006/10/shinwa-susanowos-visit/),
Susanowo replied: “Owing to the sincerity of my intentions I have, in begetting
children, gotten delicate females. Judging from this, I have undoubtedly gained
the victory!”

Growing arrogant, Susanowo started behaving rudely. He destroyed the divisions
Amaterasu had set up between the rice fields. He then filled the ditches and
ended up leaving excrement in the New Palace where Amaterasu stored the crops.
Amaterasu thought that her brother had no bad intentions and acted only on a
whim. However, Susanowo didn't hold back and kept doing evil deeds. Amaterasu
couldn't bear him anymore when he flayed a piebald pony of heaven and flung it
through a hole in the ceiling of the weaving hall, where she was weaving _kami_
garments.

She immediately retreated into the cave called _Amano-iwato_ 天岩戸,
“Heavenly-Rock-Dwelling,” and blocked the entrance with a huge boulder. As she
was the goddess of the sun, her seclusion caused the universe, Heaven and Earth,
to dwell in uninterrupted darkness.

The eight hundred _kami_ (eighty in the _Nihonshoki_) grew seriously worried.
They assembled on the bank of the Tranquil River of Heaven, _Ameno-yasukaha_,
and discussed a way to coax Amaterasu out. Then _Omoi-kane_ (no-kami) 思兼神,
the Thought-Includer, hatched a plan and asked the other _kami_ to help him
perform a ceremony.

He first gathered the singing birds of darkness and made them sing together. He
then collected rocks from the heavenly river and iron from the heavenly mines,
and called forth the smith _Amatsumara_. He charged _Ishikoridome_ with making
the eight-foot mirror 八咫鏡 (_Yata-no-kagami_), and _Tamanoya_ with making 500
_Yasaka_ beads. He also asked _Amenokoyane_ and _Futotama_ to bring a stag's
shoulder blade from Mount _Kagu_ of heaven, as well as a
[_sakaki_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakaki) tree with 500 branches.

He placed the _sakaki_ tree in front of _Amano-iwato_ and hung the _Yasaka_
jewels and the other offerings on its branches. He hung the eight-foot mirror in
the middle branches, asked _Amenokoyane_ to recite liturgies, and told
_Amenota-jikarawo_ to hide beside the entrance of the cave. Meanwhile,
_Ameno-uzume_, the Heavenly-Alarming-Deity, adorned herself with various plants
from _Kagu_, making a sash, a headdress, and posies around her hands. She then
started dancing on a sounding wooden container, pulling at her nipples and
pushing down her skirt until some of her private parts showed.

The deities' laughter reached Amaterasu's ears on the other side of the rock.
She grew curious and decided to peek outside. “Methought that owing to my
retirement the Plain of Heaven would be dark, and likewise the Earth would all
be dark: how then is it that Ameno-uzume makes merry, and that likewise the
eight hundred myriad Deities all laugh?” she asked from inside.

“We rejoice and are glad because there is a Deity more illustrious than Thou,”
replied _Ameno-uzume_.

Amaterasu moved the rock blocking the entrance to catch a glimpse of the
outside. _Amenokoyane_ and _Futotama_ directed the mirror toward her, and she
was dazzled by her own “dawn” rays of light. _Amenota-jikarawo_ seized the
moment to pull her out of the cave. “Thou must not go back further in than
this!” he told Amaterasu. So she came out and brought light back to the world.

The eight hundred deities decided to punish Susanowo and expel him. They imposed
a fine on him, shaved his beard, and pulled out his nails.

Susanowo sought food from _Ohogetsu-hime_ (no-kami) 大宜都比売神,
“Princess-of-Great-Food,” and she didn't turn him down, despite his evil deeds.
She made him all sorts of dishes from the dainty things she took out of her nose
and mouth. However, Susanowo assumed that everything she offered him was her
bodily waste, and killed her.

After her death, silkworms were born from her head, rice seeds from her eyes,
millet from her ears, small beans from her nose, barley from her private parts,
and large beans from her bottom. Susanowo took the grains and beans and used
them as seeds.
