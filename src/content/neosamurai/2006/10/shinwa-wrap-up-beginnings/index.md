In the previous entries, I went over all the names of the _Kami_ and places in
both romaji and kanji, as well as their translations according to Hall
Chamberlain. However, being exhaustive and thorough can come at the expense of
remembering the key parts and events. So I decided to write a short entry going
over the events so far, to make the chronicles easier to follow, for me as well
as anyone who takes the trouble to read my writings!

Well, it all started before the creation of the universe, when Yin and Yang
weren't yet separated. The clean, pure elements of the universe created the
heavens, whereas the murky components formed the earth afterwards. Divine
entities, called _Kami_, were born spontaneously between heaven and earth. Five
_Kami_ appeared first, then disappeared for unknown reasons. The “Seven _Kami_
Generations” followed, with each of the last five generations having a _Kami_
and a _Megami_, a kind of Japanese goddess. The two deities in the last
generation were _Izanagi_, the male deity, and _Izanami_, the female deity.

They were sent by the other _Kami_ to create the land and populate it. They
stood on the bridge between heaven and earth and stirred with a holy spear until
an island was created. They descended to populate it. _Izanami_ invited
_Izanagi_ to join her in divine intercourse to give birth to other deities and
lands. However, their first child was a failure, and so was their second try.
They investigated the matter with the other _Kami_ and learned the truth: for
intercourse to be successful, _Izanagi_, the male, had to call on _Izanami_
first. So they did, and gave birth to the Japanese archipelago as well as a
handful of deities.

_Izanami_ bore _Kagutsushi_, the spirit of fire, whose birth burned her
genitals. She fell sick and died soon afterwards, and was sent to _Yomi_, the
underground realm of Hades. _Izanagi_ couldn't stand being separated from his
partner, so he slew his son _Kagutsushi_ and went to the land of _Yomi_ to bring
her back. She wouldn't show him her face, for fear of disgusting him, but he
insisted and followed her deep into _Yomi_ until he caught a glimpse of her
rotting body. She was furious with him for disobeying her and sent her evil
troops after him. He showed great courage and drove them back, prompting her to
follow him herself. He made it out of _Yomi_, blocking the entrance with a huge
boulder and leaving _Izanami_ on the other side.

Afterwards, he went to a river to purify his body of the filth of _Yomi_. Many
_Kami_ were born from the clothes and items he took off while cleansing his
body, but the most notable were _Amaterasu_, the goddess of the sun, _Susanowo_,
the god of wind, and _Tsukiyomi_, the god of the moon.
