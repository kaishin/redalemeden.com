Tonight I am going over the _kotowaza_ (Japanese proverbs) that convey the
meaning of "anyone can make mistakes."

- 猿も木から落ちる (_Saru mo ki kara ochiru_): This one translates directly as
  "Even monkeys fall from trees," meaning that even experts make mistakes in
  their own fields.

- こうぼうも筆の誤り (_Koubou mo fude no ayamari_): _Koubou_ was a Buddhist
  priest famous for his calligraphy. The _kotowaza_ means literally "Even
  _Koubou_ made mistakes with his brush."

- 河童も川流れ (_Kappa mo kawa nagare_): Well, this one is quite interesting
  because it is distinctively Japanese. It means literally "Even _kappa_ can be
  swept away by the current." And since _kappa_ are unique to Japanese culture,
  this one is more appealing to me. But wait! What is a _kappa_? I am too sleepy
