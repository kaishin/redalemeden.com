If you check my archives, you will notice that Neo Samurai started in May 2006.
The truth is that I deliberately deleted all the previous entries. My blog has
been around for over a year and a half, and even though it is very different
from what it was in the beginning, it is still the same web address, the same
Kaishin, the same Japan… I still remember very well the entry I wrote on
December 29, 2005, and here I am writing another that won't be much different.

I am still standing in the middle of nowhere, or maybe my progress is
unnoticeable against the vastness of the desert. With nothing but the sun to
guide me, I kept walking for days that added up to weeks, that added up to
months, only to find myself kneeling down and contemplating the horizon ahead of
me. Will I find the oasis ahead, or is it just a mirage that'll disappear as
soon as I get there? I am sweating, thirsty, afraid of the unknown, with no one
to lend a hand in this huge wilderness. I can't go back, for I'll die of thirst
before reaching my starting point. The footsteps I left behind have been buried
under the sand dunes, leaving me with no choice but to move forward. The blue
sky stretches above my head, as if to witness my journey and watch over its
outcome. Following the sun might be my only way out, but staring straight at it
could cost me my sight.

I stand up again, determined to reach the end of the path I have chosen: the
path of the sun. Even though I don't know what lies ahead, I am ready to stand
firm and deal with it, be it my downfall or my glory. This is my journey after
all. The choice is all mine, and so will be the outcome.

_Kaishin, the lost samurai_
