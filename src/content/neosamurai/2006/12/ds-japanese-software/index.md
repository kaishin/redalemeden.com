Can you read Japanese? If not, save yourself some time and stop reading here. Do
you own a Nintendo DS? You'd better read my
[entry on Japundit](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106085938/http://japundit.com/archives/2006/12/21/4526/)
if you haven't caught on to the buzz. If you can answer yes to both questions,
consider yourself lucky: you've got a good selection of games, or more
accurately software, to keep you entertained during the cold winter days.

In Japan, game developers seem to be taking advantage of the huge success of
Nintendo's handheld, coming up with all kinds of concepts and ideas for adult
gamers. The games range from simple brain training programs to cooking games,
with dictionaries, quizzes, and stock trading along the way. What follows is
actually my wishlist, but it's also a good reference for anyone wanting to take
full advantage of their DS this Christmas season:

**カブトレ (_Kabutore_):** A stock trading simulation by the creator of Metal
Gear Solid himself, Hideo Kojima. The game offers an opportunity to learn some
basics of the Japanese stock exchange. Q-taro also has an entry about it.
([Buy it from Amazon.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000IUCXMU))

**こころを休める大人の塗り絵DS (_Kokoro Wo Yasumeru Otona No Nurie DS_):** A
painting and coloring book where players stare at a colorful image and try to
recreate its colors on a monochrome copy. Check out
[NCSX](https://web.archive.org/web/20061120034249/http://ncsx.wordpress.com/2006/08/30/nintendo-ds-coloring-book/)
for further details.
([Buy it from Amazon.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000FISQNU))

**200万人の漢検 (_Nihyaku Banjin No Kanken_):** If you're an advanced kanji
learner, this is the perfect software for you. It includes kanji tests, quizzes,
stroke orders, multiple-choice questions, and a load of other features to help
you improve your kanji writing and reading. A must-have!
([Buy it from Amazon.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000HCFJHK))

**漢字そのまま DS楽引辞典 (_Kanji Sono Mama DS Rakubiki Jiten_):** A
Japanese-English dictionary with tons of features such as handwritten kanji
recognition, stroke order, and quizzes. It was originally designed for Japanese
people learning English, so it includes only English pronunciation, but it can
be used the other way around as well.
([Get it from Amazon.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000EPTZRK))

For those further along in their quest to master _nihongo_, here are three
programs that can help strengthen grammar and vocabulary:

- [正しい日本語DS](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000KHRCJU) (_Tadashii Nihongo
  DS_)
- [金田一先生の日本語レッスン](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000LAM8K4)
  (_Kindaichi No Nihongo Lesson_)
- [クイズ!日本語王](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000IUE2YC) (_Quiz! Nihongo Ou_)

**DS時雨殿 (_DS Shigure Dono_):** A program that helps players study and
memorize haiku poems and their accompanying artwork. Check out
[JapanProbe's entry](https://web.archive.org/web/20071023083706/http://www.japanprobe.com/?p=833)
for further details.
([Buy it from Amazon.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000KN69HA))

**[絶対音感トレーニング](https://www.success-corp.co.jp/software/ds/onkan/) DS
(_Zettai Onkan Toreiningu DS_):** A musical game that offers several music
theory lessons, as well as pitch quizzes to test your musical skills. It
features a beetle mascot that adds a fair amount of cuteness. The interface is
cute and colorful overall.
([Buy it from Amazon.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000IU8X2E))

**しゃべる!DSお料理ナビ (_Shaberu! DS O Ryouri Nabi_):** An excellent cookbook
program that was a hit in Japan, selling more than 120,000 copies in its first
week. It features recipes of all kinds, with explanations, pictures, and
everything you might need to prepare a delicious meal. Well, except the real
ingredients, of course!
([Buy it from Amazon.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000ESH6DW))

**[ご当地検定DS](https://web.archive.org/web/20061222155918/http://www.spike.co.jp/gotouchi/)
(_Gotouchi Kentei DS_):** Think you know Japanese prefectures, their local
culture and customs? Here's the game that'll make you realize how much you
didn't know about Japan. _Obaasan_ will teach you plenty about Japanese
prefectures, then test how much of her ramblings you've understood. It features
voice recognition and manual character input.
([Buy it from Amazon.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000JKT7CS))

And here is the number one on my wishlist…

## 日本の歴史DS

[_Nihon No Rekishi DS_](https://web.archive.org/web/20070119184256/http://iek.on.arena.ne.jp/rankujunds/history.html)
(Japanese History DS)! A good educational program about Japanese history and
historical figures ranging from Abe Seimei to Takeda Shingen, as well as
contemporary figures. A must-have if you're into Japanese history!
([Buy it from Amazon.jp](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B000IZOTV8))
