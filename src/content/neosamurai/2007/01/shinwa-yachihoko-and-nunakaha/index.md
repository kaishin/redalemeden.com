Now that I've taken a breather, it's time to resume my
[_Shinwa_ chronicles](https://web.archive.org/web/20070518232904/http://neosamurai.blogspot.com:80/2006/12/shinwa-round-up.html).
I stopped in
[_Ookuninushi_'s arc](https://web.archive.org/web/20070518232914/http://neosamurai.blogspot.com:80/2006/12/shinwa-wrap-up-valiant-oonamuji.html)
(大国主),
[when he returned from the Land of _Yomi_](https://web.archive.org/web/20070518232820/http://neosamurai.blogspot.com:80/2006/12/shinwa-ohokuninushis-victory.html)
and terrorized his 80 brothers as advised by _Susanowo_. He had a child with his
new bride, _Suseri-bime_ (スセリビメ), and resumed making the land, a task that
had been halted since
[the death of _Izanami_](/neosamurai/2006/09/shinwa-slaying-of-kagutsushi/).

[_Yagami-hime_](/neosamurai/2006/11/shinwa-oonamujis-hardships/) (ヤガミ姫), the
beautiful princess of _Inaba_ who had her eye on _Ookuninushi_ and agreed to
marry him, was jealous of his official bride, _Suseri-bime_. So she abducted
their child and stuck it on a tree branch, then fled to her parents' home. The
baby was thus named _Kinomata (no-kami)_ 木之又神, meaning “Tree-Fork-Deity”.
From that point on, _Ookuninushi_ was given the alias _Yachihoko_, an
abbreviation of _Hachi-Sen-Hoko-Kami_ (八千矛神), or “One Thousand Spear
_Kami_”.

_Yachihoko_ packed and left for the land of _Koshi_, where an attractive maiden
called _Nunakaha-Hime_ ヌナカハヒメ（沼河比売） was rumored to dwell. When he
arrived, he met her, and they exchanged love poems. _Suseri-bime_ felt immense
jealousy when her groom left her behind. Aware of her grief and distressed by
it, he composed a song on his way back from
[Izumo province](https://web.archive.org/web/20070621221523/http://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/0b/Japan_prov_map_izumo.PNG)
to soothe her. Once he had uttered his well-chosen words from horseback in front
of her, she offered him a cup of saké and sang:

> “Oh! Thou, my dear _Ookuninushi_ indeed, being a man, probably hast on the
> various islands that thou seest, and on every beach that thou lookest on, a
> wife like the young herbs. But as for me alas! being a woman, I have no man
> except thee; I have no spouse except thee.”

**Notes:**

- _Nunakawa-hime_ is deified in Nagawa Shrine, located in Itoigawa-shi, Niigata
  prefecture.

  [_Ookuninushi_](https://web.archive.org/web/20070518232820/http://neosamurai.blogspot.com:80/2006/12/shinwa-ohokuninushis-victory.html)
