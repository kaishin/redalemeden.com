光陰矢のごとし (_Kouin ya no gotoshi_). 光陰 (_Kouin_) is an old synonym
for 時間 (_Jikan_), which means “time.” It is a compound of two
kanji: 光 (_Hikari_), meaning “light,” and 陰 (_Kage_), meaning “shadow.”

矢 (_Ya_) means “arrow,” and ごとし (_Gotoshi_), usually written as 如し, means
“like” or “as if.”

I guess that’s enough to figure out the meaning of this _kotowaza_…
